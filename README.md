# Lamborghini Financial & Operational Analysis
### FY2022–FY2025

> An independent financial analysis of Automobili Lamborghini S.p.A., examining revenue growth, vehicle deliveries, unit economics, operating profitability, and margin development.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the financial and operational performance of **Automobili Lamborghini S.p.A.** over fiscal years **2022–2025**.

The objective is to understand how Lamborghini's ultra-luxury business model generates growth and profitability despite relatively low vehicle volumes.

Rather than focusing only on total revenue, the analysis examines the relationship between:

- Revenue
- Vehicle deliveries
- Revenue per vehicle
- Operating profit
- Operating profit per vehicle
- Operating margin
- Year-over-year growth

The project combines **Excel-based financial modeling, Python data visualization, and written financial analysis**.

---

## 🎯 Research Question

### How has Lamborghini generated revenue growth between FY2022 and FY2025, and why did operating profitability weaken in FY2025 despite record revenue?

This question is particularly relevant to Lamborghini because the company operates a low-volume, ultra-premium automotive model where **pricing power, product mix, customization, and profitability per vehicle** can be more important than maximizing unit volume.

---

# 📊 Key Findings

### 1. Strong Revenue Expansion

Lamborghini's revenue increased from approximately:

**€2.38 billion → €3.20 billion**

between FY2022 and FY2025.

Revenue growth was strongest during FY2023 and FY2024 before slowing significantly in FY2025.

| Fiscal Year | Revenue (€M) | YoY Growth |
|---|---:|---:|
| FY2022 | 2,380 | 21.9%* |
| FY2023 | 2,660 | 11.8% |
| FY2024 | 3,095 | 16.4% |
| FY2025 | 3,200 | 3.4% |

\* FY2022 growth is calculated against FY2021.

---

### 2. Vehicle Deliveries Approached a Plateau

Vehicle deliveries increased from:

**9,233 → 10,747 vehicles**

between FY2022 and FY2025.

However, delivery growth slowed considerably:

| Fiscal Year | Vehicles Delivered | YoY Growth |
|---|---:|---:|
| FY2022 | 9,233 | 9.9%* |
| FY2023 | 10,112 | 9.5% |
| FY2024 | 10,687 | 5.7% |
| FY2025 | 10,747 | 0.6% |

\* FY2022 growth is calculated against FY2021.

The near-flat delivery volume in FY2025 demonstrates that Lamborghini's revenue growth increasingly depends on **value per vehicle rather than unit expansion**.

---

### 3. Revenue per Vehicle Increased

Revenue per vehicle increased from approximately:

**€257.8k → €297.8k**

between FY2022 and FY2025.

This represents approximately **15.5% growth** in revenue generated per delivered vehicle.

| Fiscal Year | Revenue per Vehicle (€k) |
|---|---:|
| FY2022 | 257.8 |
| FY2023 | 263.1 |
| FY2024 | 289.6 |
| FY2025 | 297.8 |

This supports the view that **premium pricing, customization, and product mix** are important contributors to Lamborghini's top-line performance.

---

### 4. Operating Profit Reached a Peak in FY2024

Operating profit increased from:

**€614M in FY2022 → €835M in FY2024**

before declining to:

**€768M in FY2025**.

| Fiscal Year | Operating Profit (€M) | YoY Growth |
|---|---:|---:|
| FY2022 | 614 | 42.1%* |
| FY2023 | 723 | 17.8% |
| FY2024 | 835 | 15.5% |
| FY2025 | 768 | -8.0% |

\* FY2022 operating-profit growth is calculated against FY2021.

---

### 5. FY2025 Revealed Margin Compression

The most important finding in the analysis is the divergence between revenue and operating profit in FY2025.

From FY2024 to FY2025:

| Indicator | Change |
|---|---:|
| Revenue | **+3.4%** |
| Vehicles Delivered | **+0.6%** |
| Revenue per Vehicle | **+2.8%** |
| Operating Profit | **-8.0%** |
| Operating Profit per Vehicle | **-8.5%** |
| Operating Margin | **27.0% → 24.0%** |

This indicates that Lamborghini continued to generate higher revenue per vehicle, but converted less of that revenue into operating profit.

In financial terms, **pricing/yield remained resilient while operating profitability weakened**.

---

# 📈 Unit Economics

The project analyzes two important unit-level indicators.

### Revenue per Vehicle

\[
Revenue\ per\ Vehicle =
\frac{Net\ Revenue}{Vehicles\ Delivered}
\]

### Operating Profit per Vehicle

\[
Operating\ Profit\ per\ Vehicle =
\frac{Operating\ Profit}{Vehicles\ Delivered}
\]

These metrics provide additional insight that cannot be obtained from total revenue alone.

For example, in FY2025:

- Revenue per vehicle increased to approximately **€297.8k**
- Operating profit per vehicle decreased to approximately **€71.5k**

This creates an important analytical distinction:

> Lamborghini generated more revenue from each vehicle, but less operating profit from each vehicle.

---

# 💰 Operating Margin

Operating margin measures how much operating profit Lamborghini generates from each euro of revenue.

\[
Operating\ Margin =
\frac{Operating\ Profit}{Revenue}\times100
\]

| Fiscal Year | Operating Margin |
|---|---:|
| FY2022 | 25.8% |
| FY2023 | 27.2% |
| FY2024 | 27.0% |
| FY2025 | 24.0% |

The decline from **27.0% to 24.0%** in FY2025 is the primary profitability concern identified in this analysis.

---

# 🔎 Financial Interpretation

The analysis suggests that Lamborghini's business model is increasingly based on **value growth rather than volume growth**.

During FY2022–FY2024, Lamborghini experienced a favorable combination of:

- Higher deliveries
- Higher revenue per vehicle
- Revenue growth
- Operating-profit growth
- Strong operating margins

FY2025 was different.

Revenue continued to grow, but vehicle deliveries almost flattened and operating profit declined.

This suggests that future financial performance will depend increasingly on:

- Pricing power
- Product mix
- Customization
- Cost control
- Manufacturing efficiency
- Investment discipline
- Electrification strategy

The central issue is therefore not simply whether Lamborghini can continue growing revenue, but whether it can **convert future revenue growth into sustainable operating earnings**.

---

# 🧠 Strategic Recommendations

Based on the analysis, three strategic priorities are particularly important.

## 1. Protect Premium Product Mix and Pricing Power

Lamborghini should continue emphasizing high-value configurations, personalization, limited-production products, and premium derivatives.

The objective should be to maximize:

**Value per vehicle > Volume growth**

---

## 2. Manage Electrification Investment Carefully

Electrification creates significant opportunities for future product development, but also requires substantial investment.

Lamborghini should ensure that new electrified products maintain the brand's ultra-premium positioning while generating attractive returns on investment.

Investment decisions should be evaluated using:

- Contribution margin
- Return on invested capital
- Product-level profitability
- Long-term brand value

---

## 3. Restore Operating Leverage

The decline in operating margin from **27.0% to 24.0%** makes cost discipline an important priority.

Areas for management attention include:

- Procurement
- Manufacturing efficiency
- Supply-chain costs
- Logistics
- R&D efficiency
- Product complexity
- Foreign-exchange exposure
- Fixed-cost absorption

The objective should not be indiscriminate cost cutting, but **structural efficiency improvements that protect Lamborghini's product quality and exclusivity**.
